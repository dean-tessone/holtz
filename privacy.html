HOLTZ

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Covers the Holtz app for iPhone and Holtz Host for Mac and Ubuntu.

Holtz doesn't collect your data. There is no Holtz account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Holtz doesn't run servers that your screen, typing or files pass through.

Between your iPhone and your computer

Holtz shows your computer's screen on your iPhone and sends your touches, typing and dictated text back to the computer. That traffic goes directly between the two devices, encrypted with a key that only your iPhone and your computer hold. Nothing is recorded or kept: the screen is shown and discarded.

The clipboard is sent only when you choose to send it, in either direction.

What stays on your devices

  • On the iPhone: the computers you've paired (their names, network addresses, and the hardware addresses used to wake them), each computer's key in the iPhone's Keychain, and your settings, such as the name shown for this iPhone.
  • On the computer: the iPhones you've paired (the name each reports and its key), Holtz Host's settings, and an optional access password, stored only as a salted hash.

None of this is sent to us. Removing a computer in the app, or an iPhone in Holtz Host, deletes it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you dictate. Your speech is turned into text on the iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition, and the text is typed on your computer. On iPhones or languages where on-device recognition isn't available, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio, under Apple's privacy policy. Holtz doesn't record or keep the audio.

Local network

Holtz uses your local network to find your computers (Bonjour) and connect to them, and may send a Wake-on-LAN packet to wake a computer you've paired. It doesn't scan or report on other devices.

Remote access (optional)

If you switch on Remote access, Holtz uses Tailscale to reach your computer from other networks. You sign in to your own Tailscale account, and Tailscale learns what it needs to connect your devices, such as their names and network addresses. Your Holtz connection still runs between your own devices and stays encrypted end to end. Tailscale's handling of that data is described in Tailscale's privacy policy. Remote access is off unless you turn it on.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Holtz doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
