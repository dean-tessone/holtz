HOLTZ

Support

Holtz puts your Mac or Ubuntu PC on iPhone Duo. It needs two parts: the Holtz app on the iPhone, and the free Holtz Host on the computer.

Setting up

  1. Install Holtz Host on your computer from the Holtz website: macOS 14 or later (Intel or Apple silicon), or Ubuntu 24.04.
  2. Open Holtz Host. On a Mac it's in the menu bar; allow Accessibility and Screen Recording when it asks. On Ubuntu, open Holtz Host from your apps.
  3. Pair. In Holtz Host choose “Pair a new iPhone”. In Holtz on the iPhone, type the code and pick your computer. Do this the first time with both on the same Wi-Fi, or near each other.

If something isn't working

The iPhone doesn't find my computer
Check that Holtz Host is running, and that Holtz is allowed to use the local network (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). Some school and office networks keep devices apart; staying near the computer lets them connect directly instead.
The pairing code doesn't work
Each code works once and expires after 10 minutes. Choose “Pair a new iPhone” again for a fresh one.
I can see the screen but can't control it (Mac)
Holtz Host needs Accessibility to type and click, and Screen Recording to show the screen. Turn both on for Holtz Host in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen it.
Ubuntu asks to share the screen
On Wayland, GNOME asks the first time an iPhone connects. Allow it.
Using it away from home
Turn on Remote access in Holtz on the iPhone and in Holtz Host, signed in to the same free Tailscale account, then connect once while nearby. After that the iPhone reaches the computer from anywhere.
Removing an iPhone or a computer
In Holtz Host, remove the iPhone under Paired iPhones; it's disconnected and can't connect again without pairing. On the iPhone, tap ⋯ next to a computer and choose Forget this computer.

Get help

Still stuck, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what you see, with your iPhone model and your computer's operating system.
